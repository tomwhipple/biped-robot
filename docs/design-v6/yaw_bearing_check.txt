Note on "pelvis_v7 vs OUTER_RING: min dist 0.01 mm" -- this is the
INTENDED contact plane, not a near-miss: the ring's top boundary (world
z = V.HIP_YAW_Z) is exactly where the pelvis's shoulder land is designed
to touch the outer race's flat top face (that is the shoulder's entire
job -- see study-yaw-bearing.md section 4). 0.01 mm is floating-point/mesh
noise at a boundary the design puts in contact on purpose, the same way
the boss-to-bore and recess-to-OD radial interference is excluded from the
ring's radial bounds (FIT_ALLOW) rather than flagged as a clearance
failure.

== Bugs found and fixed DURING this review round (kept here so neither is
   silently reintroduced) ==

1. check_yaw_bearing_combo.py's first version built the two bearing rings
   at V.YAW_BRG_RECESS_Z directly -- a PELVIS-LOCAL z range (-82.8..-73.8)
   -- but assembly_v6.robot() places every piece in WORLD coordinates,
   where the actual joint sits at z ~ V.HIP_YAW_Z (386.5 mm, not -82.8).
   Every check "passed" with reported distances of 400+ mm, which is
   physically absurd for a joint whose own parts are all within ~30 mm of
   each other -- the absurdity of the number is what caught it, not a
   passing grade. Fixed: RING_Z = (V.HIP_YAW_Z - V.YAW_BRG_W, V.HIP_YAW_Z),
   matching where the carrier's boss (placed via Pos(0, y, V.HIP_YAW_Z))
   and the pelvis's recess (placed via Pos(0, 0, V.DECK_TOP_Z), which is
   DECK_TOP_Z = HIP_YAW_Z - YAW_HORN_FACE_Z by construction) actually land.

2. The bridge connecting the two skirts (pelvis_v7.py, added because 6811's
   larger OD leaves only 6 mm edge-to-edge) first reached 5 mm into each
   skirt without checking where each skirt's own HOLLOW BORE begins --
   the ring check (once bug 1 was fixed) caught 80 mm3 of overlap between
   the pelvis and the outer ring's own reserved space at (yaw=-45,
   roll=-55, pitch=-125). The skirt's solid wall at x=0 only exists between
   y = hip_y - skirt_r (outer surface, 78/2=39 mm out) and y = hip_y -
   V.YAW_BRG_RECESS_R (35.98 mm out, where the hollow bore begins) -- a
   ~3 mm band, not 5 mm. Fixed: bridge_hy = HIP_SEP/2 - YAW_BRG_RECESS_R -
   0.3 (5.72 mm), staying inside the solid wall.

Arithmetic check (not a boolean sweep, from the constants in
cad/v6/dimensions_v6.py): two skirts, hip separation 84 mm, skirt OD 78 mm
each -> 84 - 78 = 6 mm edge-to-edge -- too tight for two independent
free-hanging rings, hence the bridge (see above and section 4/7 of the
write-up).
