== Task 1: sway vs stride, the two clips Tom watched ==
`sim/wide_gait.py sway` -- pelvis y peak-to-peak per step (lateral sway) vs
pelvis x advance per step (forward stride), from run_walk's own trajectory
(new `track_torque`-style optional `track_sway=True`, additive, off by
default). "Steady-state" excludes the LAST step, whose target parks the
pelvis back at centre (x=0) rather than advancing -- an artefact of the
gait's own finish, not a stride.

bird3_round, combo gait (drop 30 mm, foot_sep 140 mm, roll 8 deg, step
60 mm, cadence 1.8 s), straight case:
  MEAN (steady-state, 7 steps): stride 31.1 mm/step  sway 187.4 mm/step  ratio 0.17

stock, OLD gait (static_gait.walk_timeline's own tuned defaults: step 60 mm,
lift 40 mm, drop 20 mm, cadence 1.6 s -- the gateD_appendages.txt "bare" row
and the pre-existing stock clip), straight case:
  MEAN (steady-state, 7 steps): stride 29.4 mm/step  sway 138.4 mm/step  ratio 0.21

**Tom's read is exactly right, quantified**: both gaits sway 5-6x more than
they stride per step (combo: sway is 6.0x the stride; stock's old gait:
sway is 4.7x the stride). See gateD_wide_gait.txt for the run_walk rows
these come from; per-step tables in the session transcript (sim/wide_gait.py
sway prints all 8 steps individually, not reproduced here for brevity).

== Task 2: stride/cadence frontier ==
`sim/wide_gait.py frontier {bird3_round,stock}` -- for each step length
(60/90/120/150 mm), the fastest (t_shift=t_swing, t_land=t/3) x best
roll_amp in {4,6,8,10,12} deg that STILL passes all 4 gate D cases (3 seeds
each), drop 30 mm / foot_sep 140 mm held at combo's own values. Sequential
descent from 1.8 s; at each cadence ALL 5 roll values are tried (one pooled
batch of 60 run_walk calls) and the first that clears all 4 cases wins;
stops at the first cadence where NO roll clears it.

**The frontier moves the needle far more on cadence than on margin.**
bird3_round's fastest passing point (90 mm step, 0.8 s cadence, roll 12 deg)
covers 8 steps in 21.7 s at 1.60 cm/s -- 3.9x the ORIGINAL combo gait's
0.41 cm/s (20.5 cm / 49.7 s) -- but its worst-case CoM margin drops to
1.3 mm (mu 0.3/play 5), a thin pass, not a comfortable one: this is a real
speed/margin trade, not a free lunch. The stock body, with its narrower
hips needing far less of the roll/adduct assist, clears ALL FOUR step
lengths including 150 mm, and its fastest point (150 mm, 1.2 s cadence)
still keeps an 11.4 mm worst-case margin -- the wide hip_sep is the reason
bird3_round's frontier is thinner, not a property of this gait family.
Sway/stride RATIO gets WORSE at longer steps for both bodies (0.18 -> 0.45
stock, 0.18 -> 0.34 bird3) because sway saturates (~150-190 mm p2p
regardless of step) while stride grows with the commanded step length --
i.e. longer strides are the more effective lever on the ratio Tom flagged,
not just raw speed.

== Task 3: is the sway the weight shift itself, or excess? ==
Kinematic scan (`v6_kin.pose_world`/`com_margin`, combo's own drop 30 mm /
foot_sep 140 mm / roll 8 deg held fixed, mid-swing configuration -- the
tightest moment of the shift, matching the pelvis solve in
`wide_gait.solve_pelvis_w`):

  minimum pelvis shift for CoM margin >= 0 (roll fixed at 8 deg): y_min = 10.5 mm  (margin 0.04 mm there)
  what `combo` COMMANDS (bias_y -5 mm, its inherited stock-tuned safety bias): y_cmd = 49.1 mm  (kinematic margin 25.0 mm)
  EXCESS = y_cmd - y_min = 38.6 mm  (combo shifts ~4.7x the bare-minimum-margin point)
  (for reference, with NO roll help at all: y_min0 = 61.5 mm -- roll+adduct's whole contribution here is
   cutting the minimum shift from 61.5 mm to 10.5 mm; combo's bias just doesn't cash that in)

**Combo over-shifts.** `bias_y` (the CoM target's offset from the sole
centreline, inherited unmodified from static_gait's stock-hip-sep tuning)
was still targeting a near-centred CoM, which for a body that gets most of
its alignment from roll+adduction already, asks far more pelvis travel than
the margin requires. Tightening (`sim/wide_gait.py`, bias_y swept negative
-- MORE inboard, which is the direction that reduces the commanded y, see
gateD_wide_gait_stride's kinematic table in the study doc):

bias_y     4-case gate         worst CoM margin   sway/step (steady-state)
 -5 mm     ALL 4 OK  (combo)      15.7 mm            187.4 mm
-10 mm     ALL 4 OK               9.3 mm             148.9 mm   -- 20.5% LESS SWAY, still passes  ** combo_tight **
-15 mm     FAILS (mu0.3/play5 margin 0.8mm marginal; -15 turn mu0.9 FELL@11.5s)
-20 mm     FAILS catastrophically (falls in every case, <6.4 s)

**bias_y = -10 mm is the tightened, re-gated setting** (`combo_tight` in
`GAITS`): all 4 gate cases still pass (worst margin 9.3 mm, still 3.7x the
gate's implicit >0 mm floor), and it also passed the full robustness suite
(mass x1.1 / 100 g payload / 3 deg tilt / all three combined -- worst
margin 11.1 mm, `wide_gait.py robust combo_tight`) -- cutting the commanded
shift (and hence the sway) by ~20% costs nothing else measured. -15 mm is
the wall: past it the reduced shift stops covering the turning cases' extra
demand and the walk falls.
