# int8 (256, 256) students of walk16_act for the ESP32 (2026-11-02)

**Goal.** Give the next deploy a (256, 256) policy that runs on the ESP32 through the int8
path of PR #196, distilled for the fast servo config (STS3215 reg 85 = 0 and reg 81 = 1,
about one tick of dead time).

**Result.** `robot_legs_walk16_act_s256r24` is the profile `v6legs256` (int8, 113 KB).
On the fast measured actuator, run the way the robot runs it, it holds **x1.5 loop gain
in 0/16 seeds** (float and int8 alike). The deployed `walk16_act_s128r24` falls in 11/16
there. Stand, x1.25, push 0.4 and walk → stop are 0/16 falls for both. The int8 net
matches the float net to 0.003 rms in action. No student walks (walk_steps 0/8), and
neither does the teacher. The image `make firmware POLICY=v6legs256` links and the host
tests pass. Nothing was flashed.

## Students

All are 24 DAgger rounds from the teacher `robot_legs_walk16_act` (512, 256, 128), on
Warp, 512 envs, with the default recipe and about 2.5 min of GPU each
(`gpu_distill.sh`, under `night/.runner.lock`).

| run | hidden | distill rollouts | val mse (r23) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `robot_legs_walk16_act_s128r24` (deployed, #194) | 128, 128 | teacher's plant (`sts3215=2:5`, slow) | 0.0075 |
| `robot_legs_walk16_act_s128r24_fast` (control) | 128, 128 | fast: `sts3215=0:2, sts3250=0:1` | 0.0073 |
| **`robot_legs_walk16_act_s256r24`** | 256, 256 | fast | 0.0065 |
| `robot_legs_walk16_act_s256r24_tp` (control) | 256, 256 | teacher's plant (slow) | 0.0065 |
| `robot_legs_walk16_act_s256r24_q8` | (int8 proxy) | the s256r24 int8 net, dequantized (`q8_proxy.py`) | |

The fast dead time is passed with the new `distill_student.py --env-set
'act_delay_types={"sts3215":[0,2],"sts3250":[0,1]}'`. The student's `config.json` carries
the override, and `distill.env_overrides` keeps the teacher's value. The teacher itself was
trained slow (2:5); only the distill rollouts are fast.

## Grades

### Fast measured actuator, robot settings (`grade_stand.py`, `stand_fast.md`)

The fitted plant runs with the fast STS3215 (dead 20 ms, tau 33 ms; `stand_candidates.SERVOS["fast"]`),
the firmware path with `standclock coast` to π/2 (#197), a 12 Hz shaper, gyro x1, and 16 seeds.
Falls are out of 16. Jitter is the goal RMS in degrees, and power is the sim proxy in W.

- The x1.5 margin needs both the width and the fast distill. The fast distill alone
  (s128_fast, 13/16) does not give it, and neither does the width alone (s256_tp, 10/16).
- On push 0.5 the int8 net (7/16) does better than float (13/16). Both are near the edge,
  so read this as no int8 loss rather than as an int8 gain. The teacher is best on pushes
  (0/16).
- In the sim proxy, stand power falls from 3.9 W (s128r24) to 1.9 W. The planner notes the
  sim's power ranking is not trustworthy, so judge power on the robot.

## Firmware

`make firmware POLICY=v6legs256` on this branch (#196 + main with #195 servocfg and #197
standclock) produces `build/firmware-v6legs256/bimo_firmware.bin`, 0x1045c0 B, with 66 %
of the app partition free. `make -C firmware/host test` is ALL GREEN for the deployed
build, v6legs and v6legs256, and `pytest sim/sil` passes. Expected `nettime`: the same
6.3 ms median / 6.6 ms max that the walk14 net measured, because the int8 kernel time
depends only on the shape. The one thing that can move it is RAM placement at boot (1 of 3
layers last time), since this image carries more of main's code (servocfg, standclock, wifi_cli) than the measured one, and free heap may differ. The boot
line `policy net: int8, ...` shows the placement.

## On the robot (not done here)

1. Flash `build/firmware-v6legs256` (`idf.py -C firmware -B build/firmware-v6legs256 flash`,
   or `make firmware POLICY=v6legs256` then flash). Check the boot line for int8, the
   placement and the self-test, and check `servocfg` for reg85 0 / reg81 1.
2. Run `nettime 50`. Expect about 6.3 ms; the budget is about 10.5 of 20 ms.
3. Stand as before: `standclock coast`, shaper 12 Hz, gyro x1. Watch jitter and power
   against the s128r24 stand.
