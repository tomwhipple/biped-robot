# 2026-10-30: why the second step tips outward, in sim

Input from the bench (2026-10-30, `hw_sessions/2026-10-29/walk2_serial.log`, carpet): in the
two-step walk (`walk.py 0.04 1.3 2`), s1 (R stance, from parallel feet) held every time. s2 (L
stance, from the staggered stance, the rear R foot comes up alongside) failed 3/3, every time
OUTWARD. Run A: err +0.24 at 1.3 s, **+2.88 at 2.2 s (rate +8.3 deg/s)**, +3.83 at 3.1 s, u +7.3
by 4.0 s, err reversed (-0.85 at 5.0 s), abort -13.05 deg at 5.69 s, -47 deg/s. Run B (+5 deg
feedforward): err +2.07 at 2.4 s, reversal ~3.0 s, abort -12.16 at 4.57 s. On s1 the same
controller built u -6.3 and held. The question: which of H1 (false tilt: the err is not a
CoM error, and the I term turns it into an outward push), H2 (the loaded rear foot drags
forward), H3 (s2 is over-shifted), and which firmware-side fix.

## Setup

`s2_lift_sim.py` replays the bench sequence on the as-built plant of `one_leg.py`
(`static_gait.run_walk`: STS3250 hip rolls, 2 Hz shaper + 80 ms dead time, integer ticks,
3 deg play on the four rolls + 1 deg backlash, mu 0.7). The keyframes are `walk.py`'s, and
they come out tick-identical to `walk2_4cm_lat1.3.json`. Timings are from the serial log:
shift 3 s + 0.6, lift 4 s `B` + 3.6, swing 3 s, touch 3 s, centre 3 s. The controller is
`roll_pi` with PR #165 continuation (kp 0.3, ki 1.0, kd 0.03, ankle +1 / hip -1, clamp
10 deg, 0.1 deg IMU noise, one tick of IMU and gyro delay). It engages at the start of the
lift with ref = roll there, keeps I/u/ref through swing and touch, and fades over the centre
move. "Abort" in the tables means |err| > 12 deg. The sim then keeps running to see the fall;
the firmware's landing is not modelled.

Logged every tick: roll, err, u, I, both feet's normal force (foot-off = the swing foot below
2 % of the weight), the CoM relative to the stance sole's pad centre in the stance-foot frame
(+ = outboard), the swing foot's slide while it is loaded, and stance-foot slip and yaw.
`--first L` mirrors the walk (L stance first). `--ctl-steps s2` runs s1 open loop, which gives
s2 a clean, identical start in every case.

## What the sim reproduces

Open loop (balance off), nominal 84 mm sole. The walk completes, and the CoM sits +3 mm
outboard of the stance sole centre in single support on both steps (margin 37-38 mm).

| open loop | foot-off (s into the lift) | err at foot-off | peak err (toward the lifted foot) | CoM at foot-off / end | double-support roll at the shift |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| s1 (R stance) | 1.48 | +1.06 | **+2.1** | +1.6 / +3.1 mm | +2.2 (toward stance) |
| s2 (L stance) | 2.20 | +1.97 | **+3.5** | +5.1 / +3.2 mm | -3.5 (toward stance) |
| bench (base controller) | - | - | s1 2.15 at ~2 s; s2 2.88 at 2.2 s, 3.83 at 3.1 s | - | - |

- **The early tilt toward the lifted foot is reproduced in size, timing and the s1/s2
  ratio.** It is larger and later on s2, and it jumps near foot-off (sim rate up to
  +6 deg/s, bench +8.3).
- **It is a false tilt.** The CoM never moves toward the lifted foot; it stays 1-5 mm
  OUTBOARD of the stance sole centre the whole time. The torso rolls back from a lean toward
  the stance side. That lean is set by the roll play when both feet are down, and the
  staggered stance preloads the play more (-3.5 vs +2.2 deg) and releases it at foot-off.
- **The mirrored walk gives the same numbers** (`--first L`: s1 2.2, s2 3.6 deg). The cause is
  the second step from the stagger, not the left side.
- With the play at 0, s2's tilt equals s1's (1.51 vs 1.56 deg). With play 5 it grows to
  5.7 vs 1.9. So the rear knee flexing under load (624 vs 396 ticks) is not the source as
  such. The source is how much roll play the staggered double support loads.
- The bench agrees that this err is not a CoM error. The 10-25 shift probe (5 mm lift that
  never clears) gave an unloading tilt of 1.3-1.9 deg over 0.5x-1.4x shift. Moving the CoM by
  ~45 mm changed it by only 0.6 deg.
- The sim does not reproduce the absolute roll path. The bench's s2 references (-1.01 /
  -0.57) are close to its centred roll (-1.1), so the bench shows no double-support lean. It
  tilts in single support instead. Both show an err toward the lifted foot of the same size
  with no CoM behind it, but the mechanism behind the roll differs from the robot's.

Base controller (as on the robot). In every sim case the I term turns the false err into
outward push. **s2: u reaches the 10 deg clamp and the CoM is driven from +9 to +27.5 mm
outboard.** s1: u 5.5 deg, CoM +27 mm (bench: -6.3 on today's s1, -7.8 in the 10-25 hold). The 84 mm
sole holds the CoM there through the lift. The robot then falls outward at touch-down: s2 3/3
seeds, s1 2/3, with the swing foot landing ~26 mm inboard because u translated the pelvis
(the bench's 10-25 note: "the lifted foot drifts INWARD toward the stance foot during the
hold"). **With a narrower effective sole (60 mm, `--plant-set foot_w=0.060`, a proxy for
carpet and a compliant sole edge) the sim reproduces the bench's s2 trace:**

| s2, base | err ~2.2 s | peak err | u at reversal | err reverses | abort -12 deg | fall rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sim, sole 60 mm (3 seeds) | +2.3 (rate +6.4) | +3.8 at 2.6 s | +5.5 | 3.9 s | **4.26-4.34 s** | -50..-90 deg/s |
| bench run A | +2.88 (rate +8.3) | +3.83 at 3.1 s | +7.3 | ~4.5 s | 5.69 s | -47 deg/s |
| bench run B (ff 5) | +2.07 at 2.4 s | | +2.8 + 5 ff | ~3.0 s | 4.57 s | -29.6 deg/s |

The CoM reaches +30 mm outboard, which is the edge of the 60 mm sole, while the torso is
still leaning toward the lifted foot. The torso then rolls outward through the play, and I is
far too slow to unwind 5-7 deg of u. **This is a fitted proxy. On the 60 mm sole the base
controller also tips s1 (3/3), which held on the bench.** The sim's err is entirely false,
while the robot's s1 probably needs some real correction (its open-loop sag is larger).

## Hypotheses

- **H1, false tilt: supported.** The err of 2-3.5 deg toward the lifted foot appears with no
  CoM motion. The I term winds it into 5.5-10 deg of outward push, and the CoM goes 25-30 mm
  outboard. That is fine on the full 84 mm sole until touch-down, and over the edge of a
  60 mm one during the lift, as on the bench. Open loop, the same walk completes 3/3 on both
  soles. In the sim, the source is the roll play released at unloading, not the rear knee's
  flexion as such.
- **H2, foot drag: present, not causal.** Before foot-off the rear foot slides 6-9 mm
  (5-7 mm forward) and the stance foot yaws 0.75-0.9 deg (2.1-2.6 on the 60 mm sole). Lifting
  straight up first (c) cuts that to 1.3 mm and 0.07 deg. With mu 0.4 the slide is 1.5 mm
  and foot-off comes earlier (1.3 s). Neither changes the err or the fall.
- **H3, over-shifted: not supported** (in sim). Open loop, the 1.3x shift leaves the CoM
  +3 mm outboard of the L sole centre after foot-off, with a 37 mm margin (84 mm sole) or
  22 mm (60 mm). 1.0x-1.35x all hold open loop. The sim cannot check the bench's 1.3x
  calibration itself.

## Fixes (both stances, one code path)

Worst of 3 seeds, R stance first. The mirrored walk (`--first L`) gives the same verdicts, with numbers
within ~1 deg / 5 mm. s1 is from the whole walk with the fix on both steps. s2 is with s1 open loop
(a clean start); "walk" = the whole walk with the fix on both steps completes. u is + when it
pushes outboard; after a fall it reads the clamp. Peak err counts from the controller's own
reference. For (b) that is the engage point, so the tilt during the lift is the open-loop one
(2.1 / 3.5 deg).

| case | sole | s1 peak err / u end / CoM out / margin | s1 fell | s2 peak err / u end / CoM out / margin | s2 fell | walk ok |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| open loop | 84 | 2.1 / 0 / +3 / 38 | 0/3 | 3.5 / 0 / +3 / 37 | 0/3 | 3/3 |
| base (as on the robot) | 84 | 1.8 / 5.5 / +27 / 15 | 2/3 (touch) | 3.3 / **10.0** / +28 / 14 | 3/3 (touch) | 1/3 |
| (a) freeze I while the pose moves | 84 | 2.0 / 4.0 / +22 / 21 | 2/3 | 3.3 / 10.0 / +28 / 14 | 3/3 | 0/3 |
| (b) engage at 75 % of the lift | 84 | 0.5 / 0.5 / +6 / 36 | 0/3 | 0.5 / -1.1 / +2 / 37 | 0/3 | 3/3 |
| **(b) engage when the lift move ends** | 84 | 0.6 / 0.9 / +8 / 34 | 0/3 | 0.5 / -0.9 / +3 / 37 | 0/3 | **3/3** |
| (c) straight up first (17 mm, the ankle limit), then forward | 84 | 1.8 / 5.7 / +28 / 14 | 3/3 | 3.2 / 10.0 / +28 / 14 | 3/3 | 0/3 |
| (d) ref = centred roll before the shift | 84 | 2.4 / -1.7 / -2 / 29 | 0/3 | 5.1 / -1.3 / +1 / 20 | 0/3 | 3/3 |
| (d) PD only in the lift, then re-ref (bumpless) | 84 | 1.9 / 1.2 / +10 / 32 | 0/3 | 3.2 / 0.9 / +9 / 33 | 0/3 | 3/3 |
| open loop | 60 | 3.2 / 0 / +3 / 26 | 0/3 | 4.4 / 0 / +3 / 23 | 0/3 | 3/3 |
| base | 60 | fell in the lift | 3/3 | **fell in the lift, 4.3 s** | 3/3 | 0/3 |
| (a) | 60 | fell | 3/3 | fell | 3/3 | 0/3 |
| (b) 75 % | 60 | 0.5 / 0.5 / +6 / 24 | 0/3 | 0.5 / -0.9 / +2 / 23 | 0/3 | 3/3 |
| **(b) lift end** | 60 | 0.6 / 0.8 / +8 / 22 | 0/3 | 0.5 / -0.8 / +3 / 23 | 0/3 | **3/3** |
| (c) | 60 | fell | 3/3 | fell | 3/3 | 0/3 |
| (d) centred ref | 60 | 3.2 / -1.8 / -1 / 15 | 0/3 | fell | 3/3 | 3/3 (with s1 closed loop) |
| (d) PD in lift, re-ref | 60 | 2.9 / 1.3 / +11 / 19 | 0/3 | 4.1 / 1.2 / +10 / 20 | 0/3 | 3/3 |

With mu 0.4 on the 60 mm sole, every verdict is the same as with mu 0.7 except (d) centred ref
(s2 falls 1/3) (`s2_lift_sweep.py fixes`).

- **(a) does not help.** The false err persists into the hold, and the I term integrates it
  there instead.
- **(c) does not help.** It removes the drag (H2) but not the false tilt. Straight up is
  limited to 17 mm by the -40 deg ankle limit.
- **(b) fixes it on every plant**, because it takes the reference after the play has
  released. Foot-off cannot be read off the servos: the 10-25 bench found joint load reads 0
  in static poses (gear friction), and in sim the swing knee torque is ~0.1 N·m with no clean
  step. So infer foot-off from lift progress. In sim it is at 31-65 % of the 4 s move (1.3-
  2.6 s, over all plants), so 75 % and the end of the move both clear it on either stance
  with no per-side constant. The end of the move is the simplest: the firmware already knows
  when the move is done ("smooth pose done").
- **(d) centred ref** depends on how the previous step ended. It fails s2 from the clean start
  on the 60 mm sole.
- **(d) PD in the lift, then re-reference** also holds everything (u <= 1.6 deg). It trades
  ~3-4 deg peak err during the lift for some active damping there.

Model errors (`s2_lift_sweep.py errors`, both walk orders): torso CoM +-15 mm, torso
0.6x/1.4x, shift 1.0x/1.35x, play 5. **(b) lift end and (d) PD-in-lift complete the walk in all
of them on both soles** (peak err <= 0.6 / 2.8 deg, |u| <= 1.4 deg). Base fails in all of them
(s1 touch or s1 lift). The one exception is a gross under-shift (0.8x): there the open loop
tips INWARD during the lift. On the 84 mm sole only the base controller survives it (u 7-8
deg); on the 60 mm sole everything falls. (b) and
(d)-PD fall there in s1. So (b) assumes the shift has the CoM over the stance foot when the
foot comes off. The 10-25 shift calibration (1.3x, and the sim's 1.0x-1.35x) satisfies that.

## Recommendation

**(b): engage the roll PI when the lift move completes, not when it starts.** In firmware
terms: on `pose ... B<side>` for the lift, arm the controller with u = 0 and no integration.
Keep the 12 deg abort armed against the pre-lift roll from the start of the move. When the
move ends, set ref = roll then and start the PI. Continuation through swing and touch stays as
in PR #165.

It is one code path for both stances, and in sim it holds the walk on every plant above
except a gross under-shift. If some feedback during the lift is wanted, the PD-in-lift variant
is the fallback. Also worth dropping: the 5 deg feedforward. It adds the same outward push the
I term builds; in run B it was on and the robot still tipped outward, earlier (4.57 s vs
5.69 s).

## Not reproduced / caveats

- The 60 mm sole is a fit to the bench's s2 timing. It also tips s1 under the base
  controller, which the robot did not. The robot's s1 err is probably part real sag, which the
  sim (CoM fixed) does not have.
- Absolute roll: the sim leans toward the stance side when both feet are down and comes back
  to level at foot-off. The bench's double-support roll is close to its centred roll and it
  tilts in single support. Same err, different path.
- With (b) the roll during the lift is open loop (2-4 deg toward the lifted foot, unloading
  tilt) and is never corrected afterwards. The sim says that is harmless (no CoM error). The
  bench should confirm on s1 and s2 with a spotter.
- Carpet is modelled only as the narrower sole and mu 0.4. Softer contact (solref 0.06)
  changed nothing.
- `s2_lift_sim.py --fix footoff` without `--footoff-frac` (swing knee torque) is in the code
  but not usable, see above.

`s2_lift_sim.py --fix base --plant-set foot_w=0.060 --ctl-steps s2 --log run.json` reruns the
reproduction. `s2_lift_sweep.py fixes [R|L]` and `s2_lift_sweep.py errors` regenerate the two
tables (about 20 s each on 12 cores, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=` set).
